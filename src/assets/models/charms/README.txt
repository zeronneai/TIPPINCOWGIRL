Optional mini-GLB per charm, auto-detected at build time:

  feather.glb  bloom.glb  concho.glb  star.glb

Charms never recolor per felt, so baked models are safe here. Keep each under
~300KB, meshes with castShadow-friendly geometry, sized ~0.5 units tall.
Missing files cost nothing - the procedural charms render instead.
